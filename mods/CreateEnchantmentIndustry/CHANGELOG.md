## Create: Enchantment Industry 2.5.3

### Fixed
* Fixed a crash when using Apotheosis 8.7 or newer without Apothic Enchanting ([#496](https://github.com/DragonsPlusMinecraft/CreateEnchantmentIndustry/issues/496)).
* The Apotheosis integration now works independently, including its machines, affix templates, essences, Bulk Salvaging, and Ponder scenes.

### Changed
* Infused Dragon's Breath and the Create: Apotheotic Industry tab are now shared by the Apothic Enchanting and Apotheosis integrations.
* When Apotheosis is installed without Apothic Enchanting, 250 mB of Dragon's Breath and 552 mB of Liquid Experience can be mixed into 750 mB of Infused Dragon's Breath. Existing Apothic Enchanting production remains unchanged when both mods are installed.
* The Apotheosis and Sable integration no longer requires Apothic Enchanting.
